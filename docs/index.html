David H. Cook, Xiaoyu Chen, Michael D O'Leary et al.. Competing mechanisms toughen bcc binary and medium-entropy alloys at 20 K. Nature. 2026 commentary, not primary research

This paper investigated the low-temperature mechanical behavior of body-centred cubic refractory alloys composed of group IV–V elements with similar electronic structures, focusing on their properties down to extremely low temperatures. Conventionally, bcc metals become brittle at low temperatures because screw dislocation motion becomes increasingly difficult, constricting plastic zones at crack tips and producing a ductile-to-brittle transition.
